Delete Your Account

Positive Konnections · com.positivekonnections.app

This page explains how to request deletion of your Positive Konnections account and the data associated with it. Positive Konnections is a mental health support app for the Positive Konnections programme.

Delete your account from within the app

  1. Open the Positive Konnections app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

This immediately removes your sign-in credentials (Firebase Authentication account) and your profile record, and signs you out on the device.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email support@positivekonnections.app from the email address linked to your account, with the subject “Account deletion request”. We will verify your identity and process the request.

What data is deleted and what is kept

DataAction
Account credentials (email / sign-in) and profile (name, photo, online status)Deleted immediately on request
Messages, workbook reflections and other content you createdDeleted or anonymised within 30 days of the request
Backups containing the abovePurged from backups within 90 days
Records we are legally required to retain (if any)Kept only as long as required by law, then deleted
Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once your account is deleted you will need to register again to use the app.
