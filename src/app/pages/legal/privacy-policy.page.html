5. Artificial intelligence (Peekay)

When you chat with Peekay or submit reflections that are scored, the content of those messages is sent to OpenAI, our AI processor, to generate a response. Peekay is an automated support tool, not a clinician, and does not make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you. Do not rely on it for medical advice or in an emergency.

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers who process it on our behalf:

  • Authorities or third parties where required by law, or to protect someone's life or safety.
